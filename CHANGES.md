# Flux Gaming changes

## An app of its own

Gaming mode and the Games app were a module of Flux Titan Hub. They're now Flux Gaming, an app of
its own on Flux Core (what the Flux apps share), with its own updates and About page.

- **Its own accessibility service**: gaming mode reads the keys and the app in front itself, not
  through the keyboard's. Switch it on once (the ready checklist says where).
- **Typing in a game**: Flux Keyboard tells gaming mode when a game's text field is being typed in,
  so the keys type there, and leaves a game's keys alone while gaming mode plays it.
- **One shell for all the Flux apps**: the built-in shell paired in any of them is used here too.
- **With Flux Streaming**: your games and Xbox Cloud Gaming in Moonlight with their art, the TV
  asked about as a game opens, and the volume keys the TV's while it plays there.
- **Games in Flux Keyboard's quick launcher**, as before (Games app settings).
- **The Games app is the app**: no separate switch for its entry in the app list.
- **Your games come along**: the games, profiles and choices Flux Titan Hub kept are taken over from Flux Keyboard (once it's updated) the first time; Xbox needs signing in again.

Everything gaming mode did before comes along: profiles per game, controller and key routes, the
trackpad as sticks or a mouse, keys placed on the screen, emulator setup, dual screens, game folders
and Xbox Cloud Gaming.
